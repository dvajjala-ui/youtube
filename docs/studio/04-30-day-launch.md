## Gates (don't advance without them)

- No outreach before 2 samples and the site exist.
- No client work before the SOW, deposit and script-approval step are in place.
- No volume/variant automation before 5 paying clients.
- Own channel: no more than 1–2 long videos/month until the studio is earning; the channel is the portfolio, not the product.

## Month-1 success

- 2–3 paid projects or 1 subscription (≈ $1–3k).
- 100+ personalized contacts, with reply rate and best source known.
- E01 long-form published; the pipeline runs from `studio/`.
- Hours per reel under 6, per launch explainer under 12.
