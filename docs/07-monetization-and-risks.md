# 07. Monetization and Risks

---

## 1. Reality check on the reference channel

- 21K subs, 10 videos, 2.63M views (your screenshot) = about 263K views per video
- This is an outlier. Most new tech channels get far less
- Do not plan your finances around a hit

## 3. YouTube Partner Program thresholds

| Tier | Needs | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| Early | 500 subs, 3 public uploads in 90 days, 3,000 watch hours (or 3M Shorts views) | Fan funding and Shopping |
| Full | 1,000 subs + 4,000 watch hours (or 10M Shorts views) | Ad revenue |
| From Feb 1, 2027 (new applicants) | 1,000 subs + 8,000 watch hours (or 20M Shorts views) | Ad revenue |

Meaning for you: starting now (November 2026), you will most likely apply after the new rules start. Plan for 8,000 watch hours.

## 4. Risk table

| Risk | Why it matters | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "Inauthentic content" (mass-produced, template, repetitive) | Can lose monetization. Reviews look at the whole channel | Original topics, your voice, real experience, varied scripts. Do not batch-produce clones |
| Copying the reference channel | Copyright, reused-content, reputation | Copy format only. New scripts, new visuals, own branding |
| Voice cloning | Impersonation and policy trouble | Never clone him or anyone else |
| Wrong technical facts | Engineers will notice and leave | Sources, own experiments, second read before publish |
| Burnout from slow production | Animated videos take many hours | Component library, fixed pipeline, 1 video per 2 weeks |
| Codex limits | 5-hour windows, weekly caps | Small prompts, batch work, do non-Codex tasks while waiting |
| Slow growth | Normal for new channels | Judge by process and retention first, views later |
| Remotion license | License rules differ for companies | Read the license page. Revisit if you form a company |

---

## 5. Do-not-do list

- Do not paste his script or re-narrate his structure line by line
- Do not reuse his diagrams or layouts
- Do not use his voice, or a clone of it
- Do not post 3 videos a week with thin content
- Do not hide AI use if the policy asks for disclosure
- Do not buy paid tools before video 3

---

## 6. Growth habits that matter

- Strong first 30 seconds
- One clear promise per title
- Chapters and sources in every description
- A GitHub repo for each video (code, diagrams, notes)
- Reply to comments for the first 24 hours
- After each video: write what worked, what did not
