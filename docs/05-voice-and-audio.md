# 05. Voice and Audio

Voice is the biggest retention factor in this format. Treat it as the main product.

---

## 1. Target voice

- Calm, clear, like a senior colleague explaining on a whiteboard
- Not hype, not a radio voice
- Natural pauses
- Clarity beats accent. Good pacing and clear words matter more than accent
- Add accurate captions so everyone can follow

---

## 2. Write for the ear

- Short sentences. One idea each
- Use spoken words: "so", "now", "here is the problem"
- Avoid long lists in one breath. Split them
- Numbers: say them simply ("about three hundred milliseconds")
- Read every paragraph aloud. If you stumble, rewrite it
- Length guide: 8 to 12 min is about 1,200 to 1,800 words at 140 to 160 words per minute

---

## 3. Recording setup

| Item | Setup |
|---|---|
| Room | Small quiet room, soft things around (bed, curtains, clothes) |
| Noise | Fan, AC and phone notifications off |
| Mic distance | About a hand-span (15 to 20 cm), slightly off to the side to reduce plosives |
| Format | WAV, 48 kHz |
| Takes | Record section by section. 1 second silence between sections |
| Room tone | Record 10 seconds of silence at the start. Useful for noise removal |
| Mistakes | Pause, then re-say the sentence. Cut the bad take in Premiere |

---

## 4. Premiere Pro audio chain

Order to apply:
1. Remove mistakes and long silences
2. Enhance Speech (if available in your version) or Denoise
3. Remove clicks and mouth noise (manual cuts or DeReverb if needed)
4. Light EQ: cut very low rumble, gentle presence boost
5. Compressor: even out loud and soft parts
6. Loudness: common target is around -14 LUFS integrated, true peak below -1 dB
7. Listen on phone speaker and earphones before export

Use Essential Sound panel, tag the clip as "Dialogue", and start from a preset.

---

## 5. Music and sound effects

- Default: no music. Silence plus voice works for this style
- Optional: very quiet ambient track under 10 percent of voice level
- Optional: tiny sound cues for key moments (a soft click when something breaks)
- Only use properly licensed audio. Keep license notes in the repo

---

## 6. AI voice rules (important)

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| YouTube allows AI narration | It is not banned |
| Mass-produced, template content with AI narration is at risk | Sources describe this under the "inauthentic content" policy that replaced "repetitious content" in July 2025 |
| Your own voice, your own ideas | Safest path. This is the plan |
| Kokoro | Use for scratch audio and script timing tests only |
| Voice cloning | Do NOT clone his voice or any real person's voice without their clear consent |
| Disclosure | Check YouTube Studio's altered or synthetic content settings if any media looks realistic |

---

## 7. Daily voice habits

- Drink water, avoid cold drinks before recording
- Warm up with 2 minutes of reading aloud
- Record 20 to 30 minutes at a time, then break
- Listen to your first minute every time. Fix tone early

---

## 8. Checklist before export

- [ ] No background hum
- [ ] No sharp "p" and "s" sounds
- [ ] Volume steady across the whole video
- [ ] Captions checked for names and technical terms
- [ ] No silence longer than 2 seconds unless it is a deliberate pause
