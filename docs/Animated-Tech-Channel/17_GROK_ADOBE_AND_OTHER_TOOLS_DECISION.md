# 17 — Should You Buy Grok? Tool Evaluation and Spending Gates

**As of 2026-11-03.** Subscription offerings change; check account checkout and model availability. This document distinguishes consumer subscription usage from pay-as-you-go APIs.

## Ranked by contribution to a narrative technical channel
| Tool | Strength | Limitation | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cartesia Sonic 3.6** | Consistent, expressive, relatively inexpensive natural narration; API for batch generation | Need manual direction, pronunciation QA, and paid commercial license | **Test first** |
| **ElevenLabs Starter** | Strong voice alternatives, studio workflow, licensed paid tier | Extra subscription; credits/model-dependent output | Audition against Cartesia; choose **one** |
| **Codex + Remotion** | Repeatable accurate diagrams; amend code cheaply; deterministic rendering | Creative and motion-design direction still needed | **Main pipeline** |
| **Adobe Premiere** | Final edit, sound cleanup, motion/transition tweaks, captions | Does not generate an entire original incident animation automatically | Keep existing |
| **After Effects**, if included | Fine handcrafted motion graphics, character rigs, camera compositions | More labor/learning; license may not be included | Use for hero shots later |
| **Grok Imagine** | Cinematic AI B-roll, moving environments, some image-reference workflows | Short clips, imperfect exact text/technical causality, subscription usage limits | **Optional, defer** |
| **Adobe Firefly Video** | Generate occasional insert from Adobe ecosystem | Premium video burns generative credits rapidly | Only if your plan has spare credits |
| **Filmora** | Rapid timeline editing, simple effects | Don't split editing between two NLEs | Fallback to Premiere |

## Checked official prices and limits
- **Cartesia:** $0 free ~27 min/month; $5 Pro ~133 min/month and commercial rights. https://www.cartesia.ai/pricing
- **ElevenLabs:** $1 first-month promotional, $6/month ongoing Starter; plan-specific commercial license. https://elevenlabs.io/pricing
- **SuperGrok:** public pricing currently advertises $30/month; check the newer unified X Subscription package in your region. Its weekly usage is pooled across features, and high-quality video consumes more than chat. https://x.ai/pricing ; https://docs.x.ai/grok/faq ; https://x.ai/news/x-subscription
- **Grok Imagine API:** `grok-imagine-video-1.5` official examples show **$0.08/s at 480p, $0.14/s at 720p and $0.25/s at 1080p**; `lite` starts **$0.02/s at 480p**. API prices are separate from a Grok consumer plan, and generated clips are short (up to 15 seconds in API docs). https://docs.x.ai/developers/model-capabilities/video/overview
- **Adobe Firefly:** official Firefly 1080p video generation consumption = **100 credits per second** (500 credits for 5s), subject to eligible plan/feature; 'Adobe Pro' alone does not establish your available credit balance. https://helpx.adobe.com/creative-cloud/apps/generative-ai/generative-credits-faq.html
- **Codex:** your ChatGPT $20 plan access is not bundled unlimited OpenAI API credit. Check remaining usage inside your account.

## Subscription rule for this project
- **Month 1:** Codex + Adobe already owned; add **only one voice provider after testing**, budget ~$5–$6/month before tax and FX.
- **Do not buy Grok** until you finish one 60-second narrated proof and discover a precise shot you genuinely cannot produce acceptably with shapes/motion graphics.
- **Conditional Grok trial:** test exactly 3 shots (4-second server room, 4-second engineer silhouette, 4-second mood transition). Score: art consistency, artifact rate, sound independence, cost/retries, rights, value beyond Adobe stock/Firefly.
- **Buy Grok only if** at least 2/3 clips pass quality gates *and* cut real production time/cost. If not, no subscription.

## Revenue and rights constraint
YouTube's inauthentic/repetitious-content policy disfavors mass-produced interchangeable AI videos. Use original research, scripts, accurate diagrams and distinct explanatory value. https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392
