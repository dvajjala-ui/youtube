# Voice Audition Scorecard

Listen blind at matched loudness and mark each 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent):

- Naturalness: natural breathing/intonation; no robotic line endings (weight 30%)
- Pronunciation: API, SQL, one, atomic, milliseconds (20%)
- Controlled tension and curiosity, not artificial hype (15%)
- Long-form listenability at 5+ minutes (15%)
- Consistency/editability across separate clips (10%)
- Commercial rights, regeneration cost and workflow fit (10%)

Reject any voice with unfixable jargon errors or an unnatural mid-sentence pace.
