# Animated Tech Story Channel — Production Kit
**Version:** 1.0  | **Research date:** 3 November 2026  | **Goal:** Original, cinematic, animated software-engineering stories

## Decision in one minute
- **Format:** English-language, illustrated technical incident stories with precise diagrams, clear narration and character-driven stakes.
- **Core engine:** ChatGPT/Codex for research assistance and scripting; **Remotion (React)** for repeatable animations; **Adobe Premiere Pro** for the final edit; **Cartesia Pro** or **ElevenLabs Starter** for commercially licensed TTS, or record your own voice.
- **Do not purchase additional animation/video-generation subscriptions** until a 45–60 second sample and first full pilot pass quality review.
- **Pilot:** *Two Customers Bought the Last Item — The Race Condition Explained*. Fictional, explicitly presented as an educational reconstruction.
- **Target:** 7–9 minute pilot, 1080p/30 fps, original voice and visuals. First build a 45–60 sec vertical slice; then scale.
- **Important:** This is an execution plan and original pre-production assets, **not a rendered video, working automated app, or a claim of having watched every video frame**.

## End-to-end map
`Idea → source-backed research → original script → script/accuracy approval → voice casting → narration WAV → timed beats → storyboard/scene JSON → Codex/Remotion preview → animation QA → Premiere sound/edit → export QA → title/thumbnail → publish → analytics → improve`

## Operating principles
- The **voice and script lead**; animation follows narration timing, not the reverse.
- Story is the hook; accurate technical understanding is the payoff.
- Prefer diagrams, purposeful camera movements, original character poses and layered sound over generic AI-generated footage.
- Use the creator's **genre conventions**, not their actual script, character design, identifiable voice, shot sequence or assets.
- Treat every named public incident as a research task: source claims and mark reconstructions.
- One polished video is a better test than five weak autogenerated uploads.

## Evidence and honesty
The video audit uses publicly accessible titles, chapter timestamps, descriptions, a partial GFS transcript/summary and public documentation. Direct frame-by-frame video or audio playback was not available in this research environment. Thus **actual editing software, voice synthesizer, precise pacing, animation construction method, and earnings are unknown**. The proposed visual and voice design is an original production specification to audition.
