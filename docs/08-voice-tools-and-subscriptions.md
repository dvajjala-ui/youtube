# 08. Voice Tools and Extra Subscriptions

Short answer: spend $0 now. Your own voice is the plan. Paid tools are optional helpers, not requirements.

Price data below comes from third-party pages. Check each official pricing page before you pay.

---

| Tool | Price (third-party data) | Commercial use | Verdict |

---

## 2. Cartesia in detail

- What it is: AI text-to-speech (Sonic) and speech-to-text
- Free plan: 20K credits per month, personal use only
- Commercial license: first appears at Pro ($5/mo)
- Billing: 1 credit per input character. 100K credits is roughly 75 to 90 minutes of speech
- Cloning: instant cloning from Pro. Professional cloning from Startup ($49/mo)
- Languages: the source says 15+ languages but does not list them. Indian English and Hindi are NOT confirmed. Test with your own script before paying
- My math: a 10-minute script is about 1,500 words, roughly 9,000 characters. Pro covers about 8 to 10 videos if you generate each only once. Test retries use credits too

## 3. When a paid voice tool actually helps

| Use | Worth it? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Fix one flubbed word by cloning YOUR OWN voice | Maybe | Free alternative: re-record the sentence |
| Draft the full script to test pacing | No | Kokoro does this for free |
| Replace your voice completely | No | Weakest option for trust and policy. See file 05 |

Rules:
- Clone only your own voice. Never clone another person's voice
- If any synthetic audio sounds realistic, check YouTube Studio's synthetic content settings
- Decide after video 1. Pay only if you hit a real problem

Test method (15 minutes):
1. Record 1 minute of your own voice
2. Try it in Cartesia and ElevenLabs free tiers (for testing only, do not publish)
3. Pick by ear. Check how each handles Indian English and technical words

## 4. Grok: why I would skip it

Plans reported on a third-party page as of Oct 25, 2026:

| Plan | Monthly | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Limited, no video generation |
| Plus | $8 | Chat, voice, image tools |
| Premium | $30 | Adds video generation |
| Super | $100 | Adds 1080p video |
| Ultra | $200 | Highest usage |

The same page says older SuperGrok plans are no longer sold to new customers.

Why it does not fit this channel:
- Your videos are exact diagrams made with code. Generated video cannot show a lock queue or a retry storm correctly
- Generated footage pushes the channel toward "mass-produced AI content", which is the policy risk in file 07
- Codex already covers the coding work
- Possible small use: trend research on X. The free tier is enough for that

Verdict: skip. A better mic is a better use of money.

---

## 5. Spending order (if you ever spend)

1. A basic USB mic or lavalier mic, only if your recording sounds poor
2. Nothing else until video 3
3. Then optionally one $5 voice tool (Cartesia Pro or ElevenLabs Starter), chosen by your ear test
