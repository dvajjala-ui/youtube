# 03. Money, YouTube Policy and India Payouts

Short version: **ads are a year-two event.** Before that, the channel pays you through career
inbound, GitHub Sponsors, affiliates and, later, sponsors. Full math: `12-money-blueprint.md` and
`21-cadence-odds-and-india-payouts.md`.

## 1. Income streams, in the order they usually arrive

| Order | Stream | When it starts | Rough size |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Career / consulting / job inbound** | After the first video that shows real depth | Often the biggest year-one value. Unmeasurable |
| 2 | **GitHub Sponsors** on the demo-lab repo | Day one. India is supported, and GitHub takes 0% from individuals | Small, but it begins before YPP |
| 3 | **Affiliate links** where the tool really appears | Any time | Small |
| 4 | **Devtools sponsorships** | Brands look at median views and audience, not subs | 10K–50K-sub tech channels: ~$750–$5,000 per integration (rate cards) |
| 5 | **Own product** (newsletter, course, book) | ~10K engaged subs | ByteByteGo and Fireship make most of their money here |
| 6 | **AdSense** | After YPP | Last, and the one you control least |

## 2. YouTube Partner Program gates

| Tier | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Early (fan funding only, no ads) | 500 subs + 3 uploads in 90 days + 3,000 watch hours (or 3M Shorts views) |
| Full YPP (current) | 1,000 subs + 4,000 public long-form watch hours in 12 months (or 10M Shorts views in 90 days) |
| **Full YPP for new applicants from 1 Feb 2027** (re-confirmed on YouTube's YPP-changes page, 5 Nov 2026) | 1,000 subs + **8,000** watch hours in 365 days (or 20M Shorts views in 90 days). Existing members unaffected |
| Staying in YPP after 1 Feb 2027 ("active channel") | 1,000 h / 365 d, **or** 1M Shorts views / 90 d, **or** regular uploads every 90 days. `12`/`21` say "2 long-form **plus** 5 Shorts"; the 5 Nov research read it as "2 long-form **or** 5 Shorts". Either way, 1 long video a month plus 2 Shorts clears it |

**In views:** 8,000 hours at a 17-minute video with 45% retention is about **63,000 long-form views**.
At 35% retention it is about 81,000. Shorts-feed views never count toward watch hours.

**Key insight (`21` §2):** one video that hits clears the whole gate on its own. So spend effort on
*hit shape* (title, thumbnail, first 60 seconds) more than on upload count.

## 3. Ad revenue scenarios (once monetized)

Plan with **$3 / $8 / $15 RPM**. The viewer's country is the biggest lever: reported CPM is about US
$14.7 vs India $0.74, for the same video. Make English content for US/EU/AU engineers, and publish
in the US morning or midday (about 16:00–19:00 IST).

| Avg views / video | 12 videos / yr | At $8 RPM |
|---|---|---|
| 1,500 (typical early) | 18K | ~$145 |
| 8,000 | 96K | ~$770 |
| 25,000 | 300K | ~$2,400 |

Indian watch hours still count fully toward the 8,000-hour gate, so local meetups and LinkedIn help
you *qualify* even though they pay little.

## 4. Policy, the rules that matter

- **Inauthentic content** (the July 2025 rename of "repetitious content"): the whole channel is reviewed. The target is template sameness and mass production, **not** AI use. Original research, varied scripts and visible authorship keep you safe.
- **Reused content:** reading someone else's material aloud is not monetizable. Your own analysis, diagrams and repro make it original.
- **AI disclosure:** required only for realistic synthetic media of real people, places or events. Stylized animation and your own cloned voice don't require it.
- **Mid-rolls:** need 8+ minutes. Since May 2025 YouTube avoids interruptive auto-placement, so put breaks at pauses yourself.
- **Paid promotion:** tick the box and say it in the video whenever a sponsor pays.

## 5. India payout checklist (confirm with a CA before your first payout)

| Item | What to do |
|---|---|
| US tax form | File **W-8BEN** in AdSense. Without it, Google may withhold up to 24%. Treaty rate on US-viewer revenue: 15% |
| Payment | AdSense to India is **wire transfer only**. $100 default threshold, paid on the 21st–26th. Finish PIN/ID verification early |
| FEMA 2026 | `12`/`21` report a new **Export Declaration Form** duty with your bank for service-export receipts (from 1 Oct 2026). Ask your CA whether it applies to AdSense |
| GST | AdSense income is usually treated as zero-rated export of services (file an LUT). Registration depends on turnover. Ask a CA |
| FX | Expect a bank spread and wire fees, so your rupees per dollar will be below the headline rate |
| Not needed | **Form 15CA/15CB** (that is for *outward* remittances). Do ask the bank for a FIRA / e-FIRC per wire |
| Income tax | On top of a salary this is likely business income (ITR-3/4). Claim credit for US tax withheld |
| Indian sponsors | Domestic supply: 18% GST once you are registered (threshold ₹20 lakh turnover) |

These rows are advice-territory. One meeting with a CA in Ahmedabad, before the first payout, settles them.
