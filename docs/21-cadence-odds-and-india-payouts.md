# 21. Cadence Odds And India Payouts

Asked and answered here: *"If I make 1 to 2 videos a month, what are my chances of a hit and of
earning ad money? I am in India, Gujarat, Ahmedabad."*

Short version: at 1 to 2 videos a month, ads are a year-two event, because the monetization gate is
hit-driven rather than upload-driven. Ahmedabad neither helps nor hurts your earnings — your viewer's
country does. Everything below is arithmetic on the verified numbers in `12-money-blueprint.md`,
`13-reference-channel-teardown-verified.md` and `17-comparable-channels-and-lessons.md`.

---

## 1. The gate, in views instead of hours

From **Feb 1, 2027**, new applicants need 1,000 subscribers plus **8,000 qualified watch hours** in 365
days (or 20M qualified Shorts views in 90 days). Shorts-feed views never convert to watch hours.

Watch minutes per view, for a 17-minute video:

| Average retention | Minutes per view | Views needed for 8,000 hours |
|---|---|---|
| 35% | 5.95 | about 80,700 |
| 45% | 7.65 | about 62,700 |
| 50% | 8.50 | about 56,500 |

So the honest translation of the threshold is: **roughly 60K to 80K long-form views**, not "8,000
hours of vague effort".

---

## 2. Why one hit clears the bar and ten good months do not

| Views on a single video | Watch hours generated (45% retention, 17 min) | Against the 8,000-hour gate |
|---|---|---|
| 10,000 | 1,275 | 16% of the gate |
| 50,000 | 6,375 | 80% of the gate |
| 63,000 | 8,033 | **clears it** |
| 150,000 | 19,125 | 2.4x the gate |
| 1,061,159 (his 3AM video) | about 135,000 | 17x the gate |

This is the most important structural fact about your situation. Steady small videos accumulate hours
slowly; one video that works does the entire qualification in a single upload. Your reference channel
demonstrates it: 3AM Observability alone accounts for about 40% of his lifetime views.

**Strategic consequence:** at 1 to 2 videos a month, do not optimize for consistency of output.
Optimize each video for hit shape — 15 to 20 minutes, incident framing, "(A True Story)" branding,
stakes thumbnail, event-promise title (`18-topic-bank-and-title-formula.md`) — because the payoff is
all-or-nothing on the threshold and marginal effort goes into the click, not the count.

---

## 3. Time to monetization by median views per video

Assumes 17-minute videos, 45% retention, and 1,000 subscribers reached alongside (subscribers are the
easier half of the gate).

| Median views per video | 1 video/month | 2 videos/month | Ads once in, per month |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1,000 | about 5.2 years | about 2.6 years | under $20 |
| 2,500 | about 2.1 years | about 13 months | $20 to $50 |
| 5,000 | about 13 months | about 6 months | $40 to $100 |
| 10,000 | about 6 months | about 3 months | $80 to $200 |
| 25,000 | about 2 months | immediate | $200 to $500 |

"The ads" column uses **$8 to $10.22 RPM** — the Education & Science median from the 2026 dataset —
against all views in that month. The observed spread inside that category is $2.31 to $19.50, so treat
the column as a band, not a forecast. The floor row is the statistically likely one for year one, which
is why `12-money-blueprint.md` ranks ads last among your income streams.

One extra rule that bites at low cadence: to *stay* in YPP after Feb 1, 2027 you need 1,000 qualified
watch hours per 365 days, or 1M Shorts views per 90 days, or **2 long-form plus 5 Shorts per 90 days**.
One video a month satisfies the long-form half of that option, but you still owe 5 Shorts per quarter,
so batch-cut them from each long video rather than treating Shorts as optional.

## 4. Your actual odds of a hit

| Evidence | Number | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| Distribution of all YouTube videos | about **65% have under 100 views**; 3.7% exceed 10,000 views and those capture about 94% of all views | Baseline per-video chance of clearing 10K is roughly 1 in 27 |
| Channels reaching 1,000 subs | 40.6% (vidIQ, 61.2M channels, July 2026) | Half of all channels never qualify at all |
| Uploads before 1,000 subs | 68% of channels that got there had more than 40 uploads; 31% needed 150+ | 12 to 24 uploads is early, not late |
| Median growth at 1 to 99 subs | about 1.26% per month | Roughly half a subscriber a month at the start |
| Views needed to reach 10K subs | median 1.75M lifetime views across 52 sampled channels | 10K subs is a multi-year event without a hit |
| Reference channel hit rate | 6 of 10 narrative videos above 90K views | Survivor with an 11-year-old account, a working SRE career and 180 public repos. Not your base rate |

Planning band worth using: assume a **5% to 15% per-video chance of clearing 10,000 views** if you
execute the verified format, which at 12 to 24 videos a year means expect **1 to 3 videos that could
qualify you**, and treat which one as variance. Craft moves the rate; it does not remove the variance.

## 5. Ahmedabad, Gujarat: what actually applies to you

Your location does not limit monetization. Indian creators are in YPP and get paid. What location
changes is (a) who watches, and (b) how the money arrives.

**Who watches.** Reported impression CPM is roughly **US $14.67, AU $13.30, CA $9.93, DE $9.79,
India $0.74** — about 20x for identical content. So:

- Make it in **English**, aimed at US/EU/AU engineers, and publish into the US evening window
  (roughly 16:00 to 19:00 IST works for US morning-to-midday).
- Do **not** build the channel in Gujarati or Hindi if the goal is ad money. That audience is real and
  far less competitive, but it pays an order of magnitude less per view and has a thinner devtools
  sponsor market. The Gujarati/Hindi path monetizes through ₹-priced courses, coaching and community —
  the Keerti Purswani model (400K subs, 525 videos, revenue from courses rather than AdSense), not the
  AdSense model.
- Useful nuance: **Indian watch hours count fully toward the 8,000-hour gate even though they pay
  almost nothing.** So a local dev community (GDG Ahmedabad, .NET and Azure meetups, LinkedIn and X)
  is legitimately valuable early — it helps you *qualify* while American viewers are what *pay*. Use
  local reach for the threshold, don't build the channel around it.

**How the money arrives.**

| Item | What applies |
|---|---|
| Payout method | AdSense for YouTube in India is **wire transfer only** — the country table lists Check: No, EFT: No, Wire: Yes, Hyperwallet: No. Expect USD remittance with an FX spread and incoming-wire fees from your bank |
| Thresholds and timing | Minimum earnings **$10**; default payout threshold **$100** (adjustable); payments issued **21st to 26th**; unpaid balances roll over; **PIN/identity verification gates the first payout**, so start it early |
| US withholding | Tax info is mandatory. **No form on file and Google may withhold up to 24% of worldwide earnings.** File **W-8BEN** as an individual, renew every 3 calendar years, claim the treaty by **Dec 10** each year. India–US treaty Art. 12 royalty rate is **15%**, applied to US-viewer-derived revenue |
| New compliance since Oct 1, 2026 | **FEMA (Export and Import of Goods and Services) Regulations 2026**: service exporters must file an **Export Declaration Form** with their AD bank within 30 days of month-end, and "services" includes software. This lands on AdSense wire receipts and is missing from most older creator guides |
| GST | AdSense earnings are generally treated by Indian CAs as an **export of services**, zero-rated if an LUT is filed, with registration above the service turnover threshold. The **18% reverse charge applies to services Google sells to you** (Ads, Cloud), not to your earnings — the two directions get conflated constantly in creator forums |
| Brand-deal TDS | Which section and rate applies under the Income-tax Act, 2025 renumbering (effective April 1, 2026) is genuinely contested |

The GST, TDS and treaty-classification rows are **advice-territory, not instructions**. Sit with a CA
in Ahmedabad once, before your first payout, and take them your W-8BEN and the FEMA EDF requirement.
It is a one-meeting problem that becomes expensive if left.

## 6. The binding constraint is your hours, not the algorithm

| Stage | First video | After a component library |
|---|---|---|
| Research, script, fact-check | 16 to 22 h | 8 to 12 h |
| Voice and cleanup | 4 to 6 h | 1 to 2 h |
| Diagrams and animation code | 25 to 40 h | 12 to 20 h |
| Edit, sound, captions | 8 to 12 h | 4 to 6 h |
| Thumbnail, description, repo | 4 to 6 h | 2 to 3 h |
| **Total** | **57 to 86 h** | **27 to 43 h** |

Two videos a month at the steady-state number is **54 to 86 hours a month**, which is 13 to 21 hours a
week on top of a job, and your first three months will be worse than that. One video a month is 7 to
11 hours a week and is the cadence you can actually sustain for a year.

Given §2 and §3, that trade is close to free: dropping from 2 to 1 video a month barely changes your
YPP timeline, because the timeline is decided by whether one video hits, not by how many you made.

## 7. Recommended plan for your situation

1. **One video a month, 15 to 20 minutes**, in the verified incident format, with a reproducible
   demo-lab in the repo. Spend the time you saved on the title, thumbnail and first 60 seconds.
2. **Cut 2 Shorts per long video** so you satisfy the 2 long-form plus 5 Shorts per 90 days activity
   option once monetized.
3. **Open GitHub Sponsors and put the repo link above the fold** from video 1. It is the only income
   stream that works before YPP, India is a supported region, and GitHub takes 0% from individuals.
4. **Add affiliate links** where the tool genuinely appears (Sentry 40% recurring, DigitalOcean 10% of
   monthly spend for 12 months, Supabase 20%) — this pays from view one, unlike ads.
5. **Judge yourself on process, not views, until video 8.** Metrics that matter early: retention above
   40%, hours-per-video falling, and whether your median views are rising.
6. **Pivot gate:** after 6 videos, if median views are under 1,000 and retention under 40%, change the
   format (topic lane, length, thumbnail grammar, hook), not the effort. After 12 videos with the same
   result, monetize the same expertise through writing, training or consulting instead — the
   career-inbound path is faster than AdSense and does not need 8,000 hours.

Realistic 12-month outlook at one video a month, executing the format well: 12 videos, 800 to 3,000
subscribers, one video that does 5x to 20x your median, cumulative 15K to 60K views, **probably not yet
monetized**, and a repo plus a body of work that starts generating consulting and job inbound. That is
a good year. The monetization year is year two, and it arrives early in year two if one video hits.
