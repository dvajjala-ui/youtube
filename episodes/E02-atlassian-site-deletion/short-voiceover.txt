The test passed. The first production batch worked. The next one deleted eight hundred and eighty-three Atlassian sites.

It was April fifth, twenty twenty-two. The team was retiring an app called Insight. The script needed app IDs. The second list contained site IDs.

The deletion API accepted either kind. Give it an app ID, and it removes the app. Give it a site ID, and the whole site goes.

Seven hundred and seventy-five customers lost access. Even some contact records disappeared. Affected users could struggle to file a support ticket about the outage.

The team sped up recovery by rebuilding records with the original site IDs. Some customers waited up to fourteen days.

A green test tells you what happened with the inputs you tested. Before a destructive job, show what the real batch will touch.
